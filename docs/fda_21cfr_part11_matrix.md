# CryoKrypton — FDA 21 CFR Part 11 Compliance Traceability Matrix

This document establishes regulatory traceability between the **CryoKrypton Dual-Laptop Emulation & Software Architecture** and the requirements set forth by the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Title 21 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 11** for Electronic Records and Electronic Signatures.

## 🔒 Summary of Audit Readiness

By utilizing cryptographic hashing (SHA-256) combined with asymmetric encryption (RSA-2048) and multimodal vision verification, CryoKrypton eliminates the possibility of silent data tampering during cold-chain transit. Even in a software simulation environment, the mathematical proof of authenticity guarantees compliance with pharmaceutical quality assurance standards.
